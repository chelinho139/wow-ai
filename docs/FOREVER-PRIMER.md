# WoW: Forever gameplay primer (Undead Warlock, levels 1–30)

Sent by the wow-ai bridge with the game context for gameplay messages (quests, gear, talents, PvP); code questions get docs/WOW-ADDON-PRIMER.md instead (see `gameplayPrimerFile` in docs/CONFIGURATION.md). Keep it short: it costs tokens on every message. Edit it for your own character.

## How to answer

- This is World of Warcraft: Forever (client 1.60.x), not Classic 1.12. Spell ranks, talent trees, some quests and the pet mechanics differ. Before stating a spell level, rank, talent effect or quest level, check it with WebSearch (Blizzard's own news posts first, then a Forever database); say "in Classic" vs "in Forever" when you only know the Classic value. Items marked "verify" below were disputed between sources when this file was written (2026-11-08).
- The player's priorities and stat order are in their CLAUDE.md (Stamina > Intellect > Spirit for this character). Answer short. For a reward choice, name ONE option and the reason in one line.
- Never suggest anything that automates gameplay, reads game memory or breaks Blizzard's rules.

## Forever basics

- No realm list: each character picks a ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Hardcore), each its own large shard. [Blizzard: Choose Your Ruleset](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302070)
- **PvP ruleset**: flagged automatically in contested and enemy zones; faction-locked (one faction per account on the PvP ruleset). Honor comes from player kills, battlegrounds and PvP quests; Rank Points only from battlegrounds and PvP quests, so world kills alone do not rank you up. Honor cap reported as 15,000 or 25,000 (verify). [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/wow-forever-pvp-explained-honor-ranks-and-world-pvp/), [Lore Forever](https://loreforeverwow.com/guides/wow-forever-world-pvp-and-honor)
- Launch patch notes raised class damage and changed training costs and respawns; details are in Blizzard's notes and hotfix posts. [Nov 6 hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-6-2026)

## Warlock in Forever (differences from Classic)

Source for the design: [Blizzard class deep dive: Rogue and Warlock](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock). Fan trackers ([talentsforever](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/changes), [theforeverera](https://theforeverera.com/en/classes/warlock/), [conquestcapped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-talents/)) count about 52 talents with roughly 17 new and 28 changed, so assume a Classic talent description is wrong until checked.

- **Pets scale with the Warlock's stats.** Pet health was changed to 2 per point of the master's Stamina (from 7.5) in the Nov 2 notes, then the Nov 6 hotfix raised Voidwalker base health by about 40 at every level. Stamina on your gear still feeds the pet, just less than in beta. [WCLBox patch tracker](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/en/buding), [Nov 6 hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-6-2026)
- **Malediction** (new Affliction row 2 talent): raises periodic damage of Warlock spells, capped at 5%. Useful for every spec. [Blizzard deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock)
- **New talent spells**: Wrack (Affliction row 7, level 40: Shadow damage every second plus a Shadow-damage bonus), Incinerate (Destruction row 7, level 40; ranks at 50 and 60), Bane of Havoc (Destruction row 5). Not relevant before 40. [theforeverera spells by level](https://theforeverera.com/en/spells/warlock/levels/), [wowforevertalent](https://wowforevertalent.com/abilities/warlock/)
- **Renamed/reworked talents**: Improved Bane of Agony (was Improved Curse of Agony), Soul Siphon (was Improved Drain Life), Fel Vitality (was Fel Intellect), Agonizing Flames (was Improved Searing Pain). Suppression now gives hit and threat instead of the Classic resist reduction. Ruin's crit bonus is reported as 20%, not Classic's 100% (verify). [talentsforever](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/changes)
- **Soul Shards**: still do not stack. The Nov 2 notes changed how Drain Soul grants shards; guides describe a rising per-tick chance that no longer needs the killing blow (verify in game). [AccountShark](https://accountshark.net/blog/wow-forever-launch-patch-notes-explained-class-damage-buffs-training-costs-paladin-rogue-talents-respawns), [ConquestCapped overview](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-overview/)
- **Demons**: Imp from the starting area, Felhunter at level 30 via the class quest chain. [Icy Veins class quests](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/warlock-class-quests). Voidwalker (level 10) and Succubus (level 20) quest levels are the Classic values; verify.
- **Leveling spec**: Affliction is the usual recommendation (drain-tank with the Voidwalker; all three specs work). If Destruction, Conflagrate opens at 29 and Ruin at 30. [Method](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-leveling-guide-and-talents), [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warlock-leveling-guide)

## Undead racials in Forever

- **Cannibalize** now restores 7% Health *and* Mana every 2 s for 10 s from a Humanoid or Undead corpse: a free drink after fights. [WoWVendor](https://wowvendor.com/media/wow-forever/wow-forever-racials/), [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/undead-race-guide)
- **Will of the Forsaken**: removes Charm, Fear and Sleep, 2 min cooldown; reported as nerfed versus Classic without details (verify the tooltip). [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/undead_racials/)

## Route 1–30 (Horde, Undead start)

Zone ranges from [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/zone-map-level-ranges) and [games.gg Tirisfal](https://games.gg/world-of-warcraft/guides/wow-forever-tirisfal-glades/) / [Silverpine](https://games.gg/world-of-warcraft/guides/wow-forever-silverpine-forest-leveling/):

1. **Tirisfal Glades 1–10**: Deathknell, then Brill, then Agamand Mills and the Scarlet towers. Leave by 10–12; staying longer is slow.
2. **Silverpine Forest 10–20**: Sepulcher hub. The quest *Journey to Hillsbrad Foothills* at about 19 walks you to Tarren Mill.
3. **Hillsbrad Foothills 18–30** (contested: expect world PvP on the PvP ruleset), or **The Barrens** by zeppelin from Undercity (Orgrimmar) when friends are there.
4. Optional **Ruins of Lordaeron** dungeon (reported 15–20, above Undercity) and **Shadowfang Keep** (Silverpine, ~18–25, Classic value). Wailing Caverns in the Barrens ~17–24 (Classic value).

Weapons the player can use: daggers, swords, staves, wands. Buy a wand as soon as one is affordable (wand skill is trainable in Classic; verify where in Forever). No shields, no mail: skip those rewards unless for vendor gold.
